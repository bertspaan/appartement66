# Real poster references

Selected 5 September 2026 from GB Posters & Merch's current poster catalogue and Olivia Rodrigo's official UK shop. `sources.json` records each product title, product page, and original image URL. The artwork belongs to its respective rights holders; these are retailer reference images for the apartment visualization, not original artwork or print-ready downloads.

Images were requested at 600 px width. Original files are retained; square retailer images are mapped onto the 3D paper using UV coordinates to omit white side margins. Poster proportions are preserved. Display sizes are approximate collage sizes, not the products' advertised dimensions.
